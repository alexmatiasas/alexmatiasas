#### Who I Am

I'm Alejandro Matías, a **Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics** ([thesis](https://repositorio.cinvestav.mx/handle/cinvestav/5927)) who got into ML by trying to apply it to physics problems, and from there it just stuck.

I'm excited about AI, but I'm also pretty skeptical of most of the marketing around it. I'd rather focus on building things that actually work and solve a concrete problem.

Right now I'm focused on putting end-to-end ML systems into production, mainly working on my personal RAG library with a Go binary and my Pokémon VGC team recommender.

Long term, I'd like to keep going deeper into Reinforcement Learning, but for now I'm more interested in making the things I'm building solid first.
